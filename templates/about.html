About Our Project

An intelligent platform designed to help students discover suitable career paths, courses, and personalized learning opportunities.

📚 About the System

The Smart Course Recommendation System helps students choose the best course according to their interests, skills, and career goals.

🤖 Artificial Intelligence

It uses Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning concepts to recommend suitable courses for students based on their skill assessment.

🎯 Career Recommendation

The system analyzes programming, mathematics, problem-solving, communication, and creativity skills to suggest a suitable career path.

🛣️ Personalized Learning

After selecting a course, students can follow a structured learning roadmap, access study resources, and track their lesson progress.

💡 Key Features

🧠 AI Skill Assessment

Evaluates the student's skills and identifies suitable career options.

📖 Course Recommendation

Provides courses related to the recommended career and area of interest.

🛣️ Learning Roadmap

Provides a step-by-step learning path to help students develop their skills.

📊 Progress Tracking

Tracks completed lessons and displays the student's learning progress.

🎉 Course Completion

After completing all lessons in the selected course, the system automatically displays a Congratulations message confirming that the student has successfully completed the course.

🚀 Project Objective

The main objective of this project is to help students make better career and course decisions by providing personalized recommendations based on their skills, interests, and career goals.
